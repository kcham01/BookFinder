# The checklist every build passes before the owner gets a zip

From the owner's document (`docs/NOTES.md` 1 to 3), their wants (`docs/NOTES.md`, `docs/WANTS.md`) and the rule:
**BookFinder's own code, nothing lost, nothing cheap, every surface as good as the original it replaces (2.0 is the
bar), then better.** A line is ticked only when it is built AND checked against the original side by side.

## The document

- [ ] Show dropdown: every Questie and OpenMap map item once, one flat list in Questie's dropdown look (tick box left,
      name, icon right); only Dungeons as a section; one icon per thing; no Advanced Search, Quests by Zone/Faction,
      My Journey or Options in it.
- [ ] Settings: OpenMap's panel look (metal frame, bronze edges, Find a place, action tiles with stars, two-column
      tick lists with icons and stars, sliders under them) as the one settings window; a column of tabs down its right
      like Questie's: OpenMap, Quests, Books, Journey; each tab's pages along the top like On the map / Look / More.
- [ ] Journey tab pages: My Journey, Quests by Zone, Quests by Faction, Advanced Search, as Questie's Journey window
      has them, inside the panel.
- [ ] Books tab beside the quest log, showing the books (Books on map's window: progress bar with reward notches,
      sets, rows with tick boxes, Show all / Hide all / Optimized route, rewards).

## Wants

- [ ] One addon (one folder, one entry in the AddOns list, one minimap button, one map button, one menu).
- [ ] The map: OpenMap's, looking better than OpenMap's.
- [ ] Quest icons exactly as Questie draws them (its files, sizes, glow, hover tint, fade near the player, minimap
      fade), in the right places on zones, continents and the world.
- [ ] Questie's quest list, in the quest log's Quests tab, nothing on the screen; the game's tracker hidden.
- [ ] "!" and "?" in NPC tooltips and over quest givers' nameplates; Questie's tooltip lines.
- [ ] Arrow and compass to the map pin, as 2.0's: the game's marker leading, gold pin with the game's arrow round it,
      parchment ribbon compass. No route lines on the map (boat/zeppelin routes and NPC patrol paths kept).
- [ ] Party sharing with Questie, OpenMap and Books on map users.
- [ ] Automation, announcements, sounds, chat links, error catcher.
- [ ] Questie-style dropdowns everywhere.
- [ ] Nothing lines up badly: every page's content inside its frame's art, nothing overlapping or clipped.
- [ ] No duplicates: where Questie and OpenMap both do a thing, Questie's only (OpenMap's off, no switch).
- [ ] Minimap button follows the minimap's shape (SexyMap and others).
- [ ] Nothing overwhelming: party objectives clustered and faint, no carpets of icons.
- [ ] Every feature of Questie, OpenMap and Books on map present (`docs/study/`), none lost from 2.0.
