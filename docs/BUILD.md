## Ground rules (from CLAUDE.md, restated so they are never forgotten mid-build)

- **The owner's standard, in their words: "The better it is the first time, the less work you have to do. Your path
  to success will not be found in a shortcut."** Pro grade, made-by-Blizzard feel, nothing slapped together, nothing
  lost. Finish each piece properly (every state, every tooltip line, every edge case) before moving on; no stubs left
  behind pretending to be features. If a phase is not finished, the log says exactly what is missing.

- **Every line of code is BookFinder's own.** Reference addons live in the scratchpad (`scratchpad/in/`), are read
  for behaviour only, never copied or adapted. Write from the study docs (`docs/study/*.md`) and the game API.
- **Data is the one exception**: QuestieDB's data, converted by our own tool (`tools/data/`) into BookFinder's own
  files under `BookFinder/Data/`, credited in `BookFinder/Data/CREDITS.md`, GPL-3. Never commit the reference zips.
- **Talk to the other addons** byte for byte (study docs end with each protocol).
- **No other addon's art either**: Questie's licence excludes its name and logo, and its icon textures are its own
  files. Map icons come from the game's own textures and atlases (quest "!"/"?", minimap tracking icons, etc.).
- **Native look**: the game's own art and fonts (atlases, `GameFontNormal`, `UIPanelButtonTemplate` etc.), no
  custom art unless the game has none.
- **No route lines on the map** (Books trail out). OpenMap boat/zeppelin route lines and Questie NPC patrol paths stay.
- **Report honestly**: nothing has run in game until the owner says so. Offline tests only prove logic.

## How to test offline

`lua5.1` is installed in the session (`apt-get install lua5.1` if a fresh container lacks it). `tools/test/run.sh`
loads every file listed in the TOC, in order, against the stand-ins in `tools/test/wow.lua`, then runs
`tools/test/test_*.lua`. It catches syntax errors, load-order mistakes and logic bugs, not how anything looks.

## QuestieDB data: what I learned (1.1.1, Forever flavour)

- Everything is in `QuestieDB_Forever.toc` as `## X-<Entity>-<id>-<field>: <base64>` metadata lines. Entities:
  `Quest` (5914), `Npc` (15564), `Object` (7289), `Item` (23862). Values over a size limit are chunked: the value is
  `~N~` and the parts are `<key>-1` .. `<key>-N`.
- `X-<Entity>-IDS`: base64 -> zlib -> CBOR list of every ID.
- `X-<Entity>-<id>-S`: base64 -> CBOR map of the **scalar** fields (field index -> value; strings come back as
  bytes) plus `p`, a bit mask of which **table** fields exist (bit `fieldIndex-1`).
- `X-<Entity>-<id>-<fieldIndex>`: base64 -> CBOR, one table field.
- Field indexes: `src/meta/{quest,npc,object,item}Meta.lua` (`keys`). Quest 36 fields (1 name, 2 startedBy
  {npcs,objects,items}, 3 finishedBy {npcs,objects}, 4 requiredLevel, 5 questLevel, 6 requiredRaces, 7 requiredClasses,
  8 objectivesText, 9 triggerEnd {text, {zone={{x,y}}}}, 10 objectives {creatures, objects, items, reputation,
  killCredit, spell}, 11 sourceItemId, 12 preQuestGroup, 13 preQuestSingle, 14 childQuests, 15 inGroupWith,
  16 exclusiveTo, 17 zoneOrSort, 18 requiredSkill, 19 requiredMinRep, 20 requiredMaxRep, 21 requiredSourceItems,
  22 nextQuestInChain, 23 questFlags, 24 specialFlags, 25 parentQuest, 26 reputationReward, 27 breadcrumbForQuestId,
  28 breadcrumbs, 29 extraObjectives, 30 requiredSpell, 31 requiredSpecialization, 32 requiredMaxLevel,
  33 availableUntilCompleted, 34 availableStartingWith, 35 requiredRanks, 36 disabledByQuest).
  Npc 15 (1 name, 2/3 health (constant, dropped), 4 minLevel, 5 maxLevel, 6 rank, 7 spawns {zone={{x,y}}},
  8 waypoints, 9 zoneID, 10 questStarts, 11 questEnds, 12 factionID, 13 friendlyToFaction "A"/"H"/"AH", 14 subName,
  15 npcFlags). Object 7 (1 name, 2 questStarts, 3 questEnds, 4 spawns, 5 zoneID, 6 factionID, 7 waypoints).
  Item 16 (1 name, 2 npcDrops, 3 objectDrops, 4 itemDrops, 5 startQuest, 6 questRewards, 7 flags, 8 foodType,
  9 itemLevel, 10 requiredLevel, 11 ammoType, 12 class, 13 subClass, 14 vendors, 15 relatedQuests, 16 teachesSpell).
- Spawn coordinates are **percent (0-100) on the zone's map**, keyed by **area ID** (e.g. 12 = Elwynn Forest), not
  uiMapID. `support/Forever/Zones/areaIdToUiMapId.lua` maps them; `-1`/instance zones via `instanceIdToAreaId.lua`.
- Requirement races mask: Questie's raceKeys (bit per race ID); Forever has the eight classic races.
- **Static corrections are already folded into the TOC.** Only "dynamic" corrections remain (per faction / class) in
  `src/corrections/Forever/*Fixes.lua` `:LoadDynamic()` and `legacy/*`; the converter evaluates them in a sandbox
  for each faction and writes overrides.
- Extras worth converting (data): `support/Forever/QuestXP/*` (quest XP), `DropTables/*` (drop rates),
  `Zones/dungeons.lua`, `subZoneToParentZone.lua`, `FactionTemplates`.

## The WoW: Forever client (facts learned; trust these over Classic habits)

- Interface **16001**. A **modern client** (retail-era engine and UI) running classic content: `C_QuestLog.GetInfo` /
  `GetNumQuestLogEntries` (modern quest log API), `QuestMapFrame` (the quest log inside the world map, its tabs),
  `ObjectiveTrackerFrame`, `MapCanvas` data providers and pin mixins on `WorldMapFrame`, `C_SuperTrack`, user
  waypoints (`C_Map.SetUserWaypoint`), the `Settings` panel API, `Menu`/`MenuUtil`, `C_Spell.GetSpellName`,
  `C_SkillInfo.GetSkillLineInfoByID(id) -> {name, rank}`, `C_TaxiMap`, `C_MapExplorationInfo`, `C_GameRules`.
- **Secret values** exist (the newest client's restriction): unit names/GUIDs and tooltip data can be secret
  (`issecretvalue(v)`, `issecrettable(t)`). Never branch on, compare, or index with a value that may be secret:
  check first, and give up quietly. Questie turns nameplate icons and XP lookups off **in instances** and **in
  combat** on Forever for this reason; do the same.
- Character names are two words ("Firstname Lastname"); `UnitName` can return the surname as the realm.
