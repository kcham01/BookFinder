# Study: Shortest Path Forever's arrow and compass, from a player's side

Read for reference only (the copy that was in this repository's old history). Nothing here is its code. The owner
wants **only these two pieces** of Shortest Path, guiding you to a map pin you drop. **Not** its route lines on the
map (the owner hated them), its journeys, routing, travel times, flight and boat icons or Nearby services.

## The arrow

- Sits under the top of the screen (movable, scalable; Shift-drag or the arrow keys place it to the pixel).
- Points at the destination from where you face, and eases round to a new bearing rather than snapping.
- Under it: the distance (yards, or metres if chosen).
- Arriving: within the arrival distance (5 to 50 yards, a setting) the arrow becomes a **gold pin**, with what to do
  there under it (for a quest: its objectives); the map pin you set is cleared on arrival, and the map's ping can
  play (a setting). Leaving the spot again brings the arrow back.
- Shown alongside the game's own pin marker on screen where the game draws one; with no position (in instances)
  nothing is drawn.

## The compass

- A thin ribbon across the top of the screen made of the game's parchment tick marks and the letters N, E, S, W,
  with no panel or border: the ticks fade out towards both ends, so it floats in the world.
- Turns with you (east to the right), easing smoothly.
- The destination shows on it as the **gold goal pin**, with the distance under it.
- When the destination is behind you, the compass and the arrow **dim** until you turn back towards it.
- Arrived: the ribbon tints quietly towards the green of a finished objective (no glow).
- Movable and resizable apart from the arrow; an optional dark band behind it for bright ground (off by default).

## Its settings (to fold into BookFinder's settings panel)

Show the arrow; show the compass; their sizes; move them; yards or metres; arrival distance; play a sound on
arrival.

## In BookFinder

Whatever sets the pin (a click on a quest, NPC, book, search result, a dropped map pin, a Questie-style Ctrl-click)
starts the arrow and compass, aimed straight at it. Shortest Path's turns, bends, numbered stops and transport icons
belonged to its routing and are not part of this.
